Questions

Asked before most first calls.

What does a fractional CMO cost?

A monthly retainer priced to the agreed goal and capacity, typically a fraction of a senior hire's salary and with no recruitment, notice period or benefits. The exact figure is on the first call once the scope is clear.

What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a fractional Chief Growth Officer?

A CMO owns marketing. A Chief Growth Officer owns the whole number: marketing, business development, partnerships and the sales process. Most of our clients need the second.

How many hours a week?

Two to five, agreed in advance, with the option to scale up for a launch or a market entry.

Is there a minimum term?

Three months, so the strategy has time to show in the pipeline. Then monthly.

Will you work with our existing agency or team?

Yes. Sitting between a founder and an agency, or leading a small marketing or sales team, is a large part of the job.
